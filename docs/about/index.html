Hi, I’m Boxuan.
Design Engineering student at Imperial College London

I’m studying Design Engineering at Imperial College London, with a particular interest in humanoid robotics, mechanical engineering and product design. My work connects early concepts and CAD with simulation, prototyping and testing.

My internships at Li Auto and elibot span structural design, Ansys analysis, joint testing, Python tools, robot interfaces and technical documentation.

Alongside this work, my academic projects explore autonomous robotics, vehicle aerodynamics, battery cooling and sustainable product design.

I speak Chinese natively, English fluently and basic French.
