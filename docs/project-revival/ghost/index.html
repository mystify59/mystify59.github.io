Define the companion before building it.

The second project in Project Revival explores a Destiny-inspired companion with physical presence, a lit eye and spoken conversation. The current work defines a proposed architecture, researches candidate components and sets out a staged plan for prototyping and validation.

Independent operation is preferred, with wireless processing retained as an alternative. Destiny 2 provides the appearance reference; this page documents the proposed build direction.

Separate the systems.

The initial plan keeps the stock levitation pedestal separate from a stationary compute and audio pod. The floating assembly carries the lightweight shell and stock lighting; chatbot software does not control levitation.

Plan the voice experience.

The researched local voice pipeline connects speech recognition, a conversational model and speech synthesis. Press-to-talk, microphone mute and cancellation are included in the initial plan.

Prototype in stages.

The build plan starts by checking stock levitation and a representative load, then proving the voice pipeline on the bench. Shell printing and progressive integration follow those checks.

What needs validating.

Payload balance, heat, nearby magnetic components, microphone noise and response delay are identified as design risks. Acceptance targets are defined; test results are not yet available.

Research and build planning are documented.

The exact computer, final enclosure and reactive-eye implementation remain open decisions. A working integrated prototype has not been demonstrated.
