Define the companion before building it.

The second project in Project Revival explores a Destiny-inspired companion with physical presence, a lit eye and spoken conversation. The system research now has a proposed shell model alongside the staged plan for prototyping and validation.

I directed the design and revisions, using Codex and Fusion MCP assistance to develop the CAD. Destiny 2 by Bungie provides the appearance reference. Independent operation remains the preferred direction, with wireless processing as an alternative.

A fuller shell, with more room at the centre.

The V3 proposal retains the eight folded panels, recessed channels, gold tips and layered eye, while increasing wall thickness and enlarging the central cavity. The focus is the shell and its internal space; this revision does not add motors or servo pockets.

The saved export checks record 31 watertight STL meshes and 31 STEP solids. These confirm the exported geometry, while print tolerances, panel retention, assembly and physical performance still need their own checks.

Weight is the next design constraint. The CAD-volume estimate is approximately 89 g in PLA before electronics and attachment hardware; it is not a measured print. The shell needs further weight and attachment development before a levitation trial.

Separate the systems.

The initial plan keeps the stock levitation pedestal separate from a stationary compute and audio pod. The floating assembly carries the lightweight shell and stock lighting; chatbot software does not control levitation.

Plan the voice experience.

The researched local voice pipeline connects speech recognition, a conversational model and speech synthesis. Press-to-talk, microphone mute and cancellation are included in the initial plan.

Prototype in stages.

The next stage is to reduce shell mass and resolve its attachments, check stock levitation with a representative load, and prove the voice pipeline on the bench. Fit coupons, shell printing and progressive integration follow those checks.

What needs validating.

Panel retention, core fasteners, print clearances and electronics access remain unresolved. Payload balance, heat, nearby magnetic components, microphone noise and response delay also need physical checks. No print, strength, electrical or levitation test results are recorded for V3.

CAD proposed. Physical integration still ahead.

The V3 shell and its geometry checks are documented. Weight reduction, attachments, the exact computer and reactive-eye implementation remain open. A working integrated prototype has not been demonstrated.
