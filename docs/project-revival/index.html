Project Revival is a personal series exploring how recognisable screen objects can become printable components and hands-on builds. Each revival follows its own path from reference and research to modelling, making and testing.

My aim is to make complex builds accessible to younger makers and give them the experience of creating something substantial. This is the intended direction of the series; workshops and educational outcomes have not yet been delivered.

Two revivals, at different stages.

Anvil · From CAD to a first print

  1. 01
    CAD recorded

    Translate the reference into CAD

    The original Fusion model separates the game-inspired form into eleven components for a nonfunctional prop.

  2. 02
    First print confirmed

    Print the first full-scale iteration

    The first iteration was printed in China and remains there. The images on this page show the CAD rather than photographs of that print.

  3. 03
    In progress

    Continue the internal development

    The internal arrangement and sound module remain unfinished. Later CAD studies explore the direction, but their physical operation is untested.

  4. 04
    Next

    Check the assembled prop

    Record fit, assembly, movement, durability and sound checks before describing the internal design as complete.

Ghost · From research to a build plan

  1. 01
    Research recorded

    Define the desk companion

    Research establishes the direction: a Destiny-inspired floating shell, a lit eye and spoken conversation.

  2. 02
    Plan documented

    Map the separate systems

    The documented plan separates stock levitation from stationary computing and audio, with a staged build and validation sequence.

  3. 03
    In progress

    Resolve the build choices

    The exact computer, final enclosure and eye behaviour remain open. The current work is research and planning.

  4. 04
    Next

    Prototype and test in stages

    Check stock levitation and a representative load, prove voice on the bench, then print and integrate the shell. No integrated prototype is demonstrated yet.
