The series

Project Revival is a personal series exploring how recognisable screen objects can become printable components and hands-on builds. Each revival follows its own path from reference and research to modelling, making and testing.

My aim is to make complex builds accessible to younger makers and give them the experience of creating something substantial. This is the intended direction of the series; workshops and educational outcomes have not yet been delivered.
